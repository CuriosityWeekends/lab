# What is Curiosity Weekends?

Curiosity Weekends is a community-driven tinkering initiative, primarily led by high school students, focusing on:

* Learning through making and tinkering  
* Peer-to-peer learning

# Genesis

In mid-2024, a group of students began gathering on the second Saturday of each month, emphasizing:

* Learning through sharing  
* Puzzle-solving and treasure hunts  
* Hackathons

By March 2025, the need for a shared workspace during summer vacation led to discussions with Linnk Academy. This collaboration resulted in the establishment of the Curiosity Lab on the first floor of ASAP, Vidyanagar, Kasaragod, generously provided by Linnk Academy.

# Philosophy

A baby learns to walk not by studying physics but through curious, playful attempts and learning from failures. Similarly, learning, skill-building, product development, and even running a factory can be enjoyable when approached with curiosity and a willingness to embrace failures sustainably.

# Events and Programs

* **Math Circle**: A gathering on Saturdays, 4 to 12 hours, where a difficult problem or concept is taken and attempted to be solved analytically, numerically and with experiments if possible. This makes math common sense and a fun activity. In turn introducing math to the society and creating math experts.
* **District level STEM Deliberation**: Delivered a session to the CBSE school teachers of Kasaragod about STEM skill development at MP Ahamed School, Kasaragod on June 21.
* **School Praveshanolsavam**: The humanoid robot was used to amuse children at School Praveshanolsavam, Kasaragod.

# Roadmap

With the success of summer activities, we're transitioning to a hybrid model combining weekday evening sessions with intensive weekend workshops. Our focus areas for the coming months include:

* Establishing partnerships with industry leaders for internship opportunities  
* Launching online courses for remote community participation  
* Developing specialized tracks in AI/ML, IoT, and sustainable technology  
* Creating a mentorship network connecting students with professionals

**Collaboration Initiatives**

We're actively pursuing collaborations with:

* Major technology companies for skill development programs  
* Government College, Kasaragod for research projects  
* Central University of Kerala for academic partnerships  
* Local industries for real-world problem-solving projects
