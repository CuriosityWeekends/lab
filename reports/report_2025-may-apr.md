# What is Curiosity Weekends?

Curiosity Weekends is a community-driven tinkering initiative, primarily led by high school students, focusing on:

* Learning through making and tinkering  
* Peer-to-peer learning

# Genesis

In mid-2024, a group of students began gathering on the second Saturday of each month, emphasizing:

* Learning through sharing  
* Puzzle-solving and treasure hunts  
* Hackathons

By March 2025, the need for a shared workspace during summer vacation led to discussions with Linnk Academy. This collaboration resulted in the establishment of the Curiosity Lab on the first floor of ASAP, Vidyanagar, Kasaragod, generously provided by Linnk Academy.

# Philosophy

A baby learns to walk not by studying physics but through curious, playful attempts and learning from failures. Similarly, learning, skill-building, product development, and even running a factory can be enjoyable when approached with curiosity and a willingness to embrace failures sustainably.

# The Last Two Months: April and May 2025

In the past two months, we established the Curiosity Lab with the following facilities:

* A working facility with a capacity of 14 seats  
* Spare laptops for use  
* An electronics development station with tools and spare parts  
* NodeMCUs, Arduinos, and Raspberry Pis  
* A soldering station  
* A prototyping station with breadboards and multimeters  
* 3D, Virtual, and Augmented Reality Centers  
  * 3D printer  
  * 3D pen  
  * Meta Quest VR/AR corner  
  * Microsoft Kinect 3D cameras  
* Tools: electric cutters, electric drills, and hand drills

# Side Projects in Progress

* ## TemPro: Temperature Profiler

  * **Issue**: Uneven temperature distribution in the Curiosity Lab led to complaints  
  * **Solution**: 10 IoT temperature sensors integrated into a web dashboard via MQTT, with calibration for offset error correction

# Roadmap

Over the past two months, during summer vacation, the lab hosted approximately 20 people daily, both in-person and on Discord. As school resumes, we will shift to a weekends-focused mode, continuing side projects and seeking broader collaborations with:

* Prominent core technology companies in India  
* Government College, Kasaragod  
* Central University of Kerala, Kasaragod

**Asks and Gives**

The community operates without formal fundraising, relying on voluntary contributions of components and equipment from individuals and organizations. We plan to continue this model. Below are specific requests from student groups, paired with their commitments, with detailed proposals available on our Git repository and community website. All contributions and deliverables are executed peer-to-peer, with updates shared with the Curiosity Weekends community.

* ## Electronic Workbench
